What customers say

Questions people ask

Something else on your mind? Email us and we'll answer within a business day.

Is there a minimum order?

No. Printed items can be ordered one at a time. Embroidery is also available from one piece; a one-time digitizing fee applies to each new design.

What kind of artwork should I send?

A high-resolution PNG, PDF, SVG or AI file works best. A phone photo of a sketch is fine for a starting point — we'll clean it up and send a proof.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Chesterfield County and serve customers across Richmond, Midlothian and the surrounding area. Orders can also be shipped.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the garment, the number of pieces, and how many colors and placements your design has. Send your design or idea and we'll reply with a free quote within one business day. Larger orders cost less per piece.

How do I pay?

After you approve your quote and proof, we send a secure invoice that you can pay online by card.

How long does it take?

Can each shirt have a different name or number?

Yes. Send a list of names, numbers and sizes with your order and we'll put each one on its own piece. It's popular for teams, choirs and family reunions.

Can you match my logo or brand colors?

We match your colors as closely as ink and thread allow, and you'll see the result on your proof before anything is made.

Do you print or embroider in Amharic?

Yes. You can type Amharic in the design studio or send us the text, and you'll see exactly how it looks on your proof.

How do I get my order?

When your order is ready, we'll contact you to arrange a local pick-up in the Chesterfield County area, or ship it to your door.

Ready to make yours?

Start in the design studio, or tell us about a group order and we'll take it from there.
