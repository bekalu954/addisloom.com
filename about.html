Where the Name Comes From

Addis Loom — Where Heritage Meets Modern Creativity.

The name Addis Loom reflects our Ethiopian heritage, passion for craftsmanship, and vision for modern apparel.

Addis አዲስ

“Addis” (አዲስ) means “new” in Amharic, symbolizing fresh ideas, innovation, and new beginnings.

Loom

“Loom” represents the timeless art of weaving, where individual threads come together to create something beautiful and meaningful.

Together, Addis Loom represents the connection between tradition and innovation — bringing cultural inspiration, creativity, and modern design into every piece we create.

Our name reflects a simple belief:

Every thread carries a story, every design expresses an identity, and every creation brings people together.
