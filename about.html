Heritage you can wear, made for the people you wear it with

Addis Loom began by bringing Ethiopian heritage clothing and goods to customers through Amazon and Walmart. Now, from our home base in Chesterfield County, we also make custom printed and embroidered apparel for families, churches, teams and small businesses across Richmond and Midlothian.

Where the name comes from

Addis

Means "new," as in Addis Ababa, the "new flower." It stands for what we make: something new, built on something old.

Loom

The frame weavers use to make shema, the hand-woven cotton behind the habesha kemis and netela, finished with a colorful tibeb border. That border runs through our whole site.
