What we stitch

  • Polos and button-down shirts — left chest logo, name on the right
  • Caps and beanies — front, side or back
  • Hoodies, crewnecks and jackets — chest, sleeve or large back designs
  • Aprons, totes and towels — for restaurants, salons and gifts
  • Personal names and monograms on items you bring in

Good to know

  • No minimum order. One embroidered cap is fine.
  • We match your brand colors to the closest thread colors and list them on the proof.
  • Very small text (under about ¼ inch) and photo-like detail don't stitch well. We'll suggest a simplified version if needed.

From logo to finished stitch

  1. Send your logo

    Any file you have. Vector files (AI, SVG, PDF) are ideal.

  2. We digitize it

    Your design is converted into a stitch file sized for each placement.

  3. Approve the proof

    You see placement, size and thread colors before we start.

  4. Stitched and delivered
