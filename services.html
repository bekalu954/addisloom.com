Full-color printing

Photos, gradients and many-color logos, sharp on any shirt color.

We use direct-to-film (DTF) transfers, which print every color in one pass and press onto cotton, polyester and blends. There's no setup charge, so a single shirt costs the same per piece as ten.

Good for

  • Photos and detailed artwork
  • Names and numbers on each shirt
  • Small runs and one-offs

Works on

  • T-shirts and crewnecks
  • Hoodies and jackets
  • Tote bags

Embroidery

Stitched logos and names that look sharp for years.

We turn your logo into a stitch file, match your colors to thread, and embroider it onto polos, caps, jackets and bags. Once your logo is digitized, reorders are quick and there's no setup fee again.

Good for

  • Business and church logos
  • Names and monograms
  • Caps and outerwear

Placements

  • Left or right chest
  • Cap front, side or back
  • Sleeve and full back
